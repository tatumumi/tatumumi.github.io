From Maunakea to Caltech

Instrumentation, optics, and a lot of curiosity

I'm a rising second-year Astronomy Ph.D. student at Caltech. I earned my B.S. in Astrophysics and B.A. in Information & Computer Sciences at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa, where I first got hooked on instrumentation as an Akamai Intern at the W.M. Keck Observatory.

My research interests are in astronomical instrumentation and optical engineering, with a focus on adaptive optics. Outside of research, I enjoy lei making, rug making, painting, and spending time in the ocean.
