About Me

Astronomy Ph.D. student at Caltech

I'm a rising second-year Astronomy Ph.D. student at Caltech, interested in astronomical instrumentation and optical engineering, with a particular focus on adaptive optics. Before Caltech, I earned my B.S. in Astrophysics and B.A. in Information & Computer Sciences at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa, where I got my start in instrumentation as an Akamai Intern at the W.M. Keck Observatory.

When I'm not in the lab or at the telescope, I'm usually making art in some form — lei making, rug making, and painting are current favorites — or out in the ocean looking for a new spot to eat.
