Mentors

Grateful for the guidance along the way

I've been lucky to work with mentors across observatories, universities, and internships. Here are a few who have shaped my path so far — more to come as I settle into my Ph.D. at Caltech.

Dr. Charlotte Guthery

W.M. Keck Observatory · Akamai Internship, June – August 2024

Mentored my work rebuilding the calibration setup process for a high-order deformable mirror, including porting adaptive optics calibration scripts from IDL to Python and documenting the new software.

Dr. Joshua Barnes

University of Hawai'i at Mānoa & The Institute for Astronomy · June 2023 – Present

Mentored my research on the effects of halo anisotropy in simulated mergers of disc galaxies, which grew into a paper in preparation for submission to MNRAS.

Dr. David Rubin

University of Hawai'i at Mānoa · June 2021 – May 2024

Mentored my project using machine learning to discover the first supernovae with the James Webb Space Telescope, including training a CNN to detect Type Ia supernovae.
